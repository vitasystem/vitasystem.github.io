`purple-tractor-mist.webp` is the translucent moving fog layer. `pt-fog-thick` is the optional denser-fog state.

The `pt-video-section` is intentionally a non-playing poster marked “Coming soon”. Replace it with a real video element once the video is supplied; no pretend player or invented duration is shown.

The soundtrack uses the existing repository audio `assets/audio/10-hto-vypustyv-sobak.MP3`. The conversation preview uses supplied still frames; the question attributed to Róman comes from the user brief. Other quotations are marked song lyrics, not invented interview dialogue.
