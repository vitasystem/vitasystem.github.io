01 / THE ARTIST

A world with
its own gravity.

VITA is a Ukrainian pop artist whose universe moves between music, cinema and the imagined future. Her sound brings together modern dance-pop, synthwave and disco-house, while her visual identity unfolds through city lights, rain, motion and stories that reach beyond the stage.

This is more than a discography. VITA SYSTEM connects songs, moving images, fictional rooms and individual experiences into a single creative space. Every new door opens a different part of the story.
